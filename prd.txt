# Overview

**Product:** KwenTech - Real Estate "Revenue Recovery" Engine

**Brand:** KwenTech (targeting the Philippines real estate market)

**Problem:** Real estate agents in the Philippines lose thousands in commissions because they can't keep up with old leads (dormant databases) and miss high-intent signals on social media. Existing tools are often "gated" (forcing users to sign up), which creates friction.

**Solution:** A comprehensive real estate platform that empowers agents with AI-powered tools for lead management, opportunity discovery, and financial qualification. The platform provides a suite of integrated tools designed to help agents maximize their revenue potential through intelligent automation, social media insights, and instant financial calculations.

**Market:** Philippines (Filipino real estate agents)

# Core Features

Note: The goal only of this is to build a POC or Mock Website for this. So Just UI with mock data and flow without real api for now.

* **AI Lead Reactivator:**
* **What:** Scans old CSV/CRM exports and sends personalized, low-friction re-engagement prompts.
* **Why:** To monetize "dead" data the agent already paid for.


* **Matching Intent Curator (FB Intent Curator):**
* **What:** Aggregates and filters Facebook posts/comments based on intent keywords (e.g., "moving to," "budget for house").
* **Why:** To find "active" buyers in the wild before they hit a portal like Zillow.


* **Smart CPQ (Cost-Price-Quote) Calculator:**
* **What:** A financial engine that computes mortgage estimates, closing costs, and agent commissions.
* **Why:** Enables agents to provide instant, accurate financial answers to qualify leads during the first call.
